I took a dancing class today after my trip to Amsterdam (which I'll share about later) -- it's been 3 years since my last one. After the session, I feel it's incredible how on earth would I allow myself to stop doing something that I genuinely enjoy? 

What stopped me was nothing dramatic. My local club was forced to close due to landlord's plan of selling the place. I was saddened by the news, but instead of finding a new one immediately, I simply postponed. And I didn't think it's a big deal -- I could always dance with Youtube videos, or de-dust my Nintendo and dance with JustDance again. So many alternatives, so many possibilities, what's to worry about?

Yet despite all those accessible resources at my fingertips, without the routines and plans, I seldom danced. Even when I did, I didn't really feel the vitality from a live session.  

What I felt today during the class, was pure energy & freedom. My teacher Andrea is not slim nor tall, but I can tell from a single look that she's the teacher. She looks like Latina, laughs like Latina, and dances like Latina (in the best possible ways), and indeed she confirmed to be Latina! 

For a long time, I've used my body in an utilitarian way (as a means to transport my eyes and brain or to fit my outfit). Even though I do maintain it by exercising regularly, it's more like paying my dues.

Dancing with her made me appreciate my body as the hero of the story, and my arms and legs deserve to be moved for joy, for their own sake.

It's not time to make any new year resolution yet, but I think I should dance more next year!
