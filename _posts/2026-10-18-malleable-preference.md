Just two months ago, I said, at my stage of life, I'm probably only left with [small decisions to make in daily life](https://yihanxu.github.io/my-on-and-off-weight-training/).  

Guess I was wrong. I'll share more contexts when I'm through it, but at the moment, I will just quote what Chris shared with me: "Your preferences shape your decisions as much as your decisions shape your preferences". 

Maybe my future self will thank me for the new preferences I form through the decisions I make here and now.
